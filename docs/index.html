Wilson intervals, not Wald: at n=9 several conditions sit at 9/9, where a Wald interval has zero width and says nothing. The judge agreed with these rules at κ = 0.62 [0.31, 0.88], and an exact McNemar test (p = 0.031) shows it is systematically more lenient rather than merely noisier. 2 of 45 verdicts came back as prose and were recorded as parse failures, never retried.
